Responsible AI & Calibrated Trust

Governance & Verification Safeguards in TestTrust AI

1. Probabilistic AI vs. Deterministic Verification

TestTrust AI strictly separates LLM-generated test suggestions & explanations from deterministic execution evidence produced by Maven, JUnit 5, JaCoCo, and PIT.

2. Mutation Score Is Evidence, Not Proof

A high mutation score indicates strong fault-detection sensitivity against common syntactic bugs, but does not mathematically guarantee bug-free software or capture missing business specifications.

3. Human-in-the-Loop & Sandboxed Execution

Developers are prompted to review AI-proposed assertions before merging. Untrusted Java builds execute inside isolated containers with strict resource timeouts.
